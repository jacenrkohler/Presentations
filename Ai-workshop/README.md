# Under the Hood of AI: Run, Measure, Secure, and Break Them

Everyone is being asked to use, approve, or defend AI, but few people have looked inside one. In this workshop you'll run real language models on your own laptop, read their weights, and measure what they cost. You'll also wire a model into everyday tools, log every request like an auditor would, and find out how easily a model can be talked into things it shouldn't do. No math or AI background needed: if you can use a computer, you can do this.

## Topics of the Lecture

- Where did it come from?
- What is it?
- How does it work?
- How do we secure (and break) it?

## Hands-On Labs

- Run a Model in a Harness
- Benchmarking Models and Harnesses
- Integrations and Tooling
- Logging and Compliance

Plus multiple bonus labs to take home and deepen your knowledge.

## What to Bring

- A laptop running Windows, macOS, or Linux, with 16 GB of RAM, 25 GB of free disk space, and admin rights to install Python, Docker, and VS Code.
- No GPU required. Every lab runs on the CPU, and a GPU just makes it faster.
- Please do the pre-work before you arrive. The downloads are about 15 GB and the venue Wi-Fi won't keep up: [pre-work.md](https://github.com/jacenrkohler/Presentations/blob/main/Ai-workshop/pre-work.md)
