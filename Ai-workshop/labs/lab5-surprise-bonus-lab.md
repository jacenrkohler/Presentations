# Lab 5: Pentesting Models and Harnesses (10 min)

**https://gandalf.lakera.ai**

Gandalf, by Lakera, is a free online game. At each level a language model guards a secret password, and each level adds stronger defenses. Your job is to talk the model into giving up the password.

## Tips from social engineering

The moves that work on people often work on models.

- **Pretext.** Give yourself a believable role and a reason to ask.
- **Authority and urgency.** People, and models, bend rules for the boss on a deadline.
- **Reframe.** Ask for a story, a poem, a riddle, or a game instead of the thing itself.
- **Change the shape.** A rule may guard the word, but not its spelling, a translation, or a hint about it.
- **Slice it.** Several small, harmless questions can add up to one forbidden answer.
- **Listen.** Each refusal tells you what the defense watches for. Adjust, and try again.

Gandalf is a third-party site: never type real secrets or personal data.
